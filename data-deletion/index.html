Data Deletion Requests

This page describes how to request deletion of information shared with AISOFT Automation.

  1. Email the official business contact with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Explain which information or interaction the request concerns. Do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.
  3. We will verify the request as reasonably necessary and respond according to applicable legal requirements.
