Data Deletion Requests

To request deletion of information shared with AISOFT Automation:

  1. Email hello@aisoftautomation.com with the subject “Data deletion request”.
  2. Explain which information or interaction the request concerns. Do not send passwords or other sensitive credentials.
  3. We will verify the request as reasonably necessary and respond according to applicable legal requirements.

Requests may be subject to legal retention obligations. This process should be maintained by a monitored business inbox.

Contact: hello@aisoftautomation.com
