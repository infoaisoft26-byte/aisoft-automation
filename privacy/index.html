Review this policy against actual business practices, providers, retention periods, and applicable law before relying on it.

AISOFT Automation may receive information you choose to provide when contacting us, such as your name, email address, company details, and message.

Website analytics and Meta Pixel

Where configured, Meta Pixel may measure website visits and contact-button interactions for advertising measurement. It is loaded only after you select “Accept” in the website's analytics/marketing consent notice. Selecting “Reject” prevents the Pixel from loading on that browser. Your choice is stored in that browser's local storage; clearing browser data resets the choice. You can change your choice by clearing the site data and revisiting the website.

Business integrations

If the Meta integrations are enabled, authorized business account connections may process Facebook Page, Instagram Professional account, WhatsApp messaging, and lead-form information to provide the requested automation. Access tokens are intended to be encrypted in the server database. Lead information and webhook payloads may include contact details supplied by people through a business lead form or message. Only connect accounts and process data you are authorized to handle.

Sharing, security, and retention

Hosting, database, Meta, and other service providers may process information as needed to operate configured features. Define and apply retention/deletion periods for lead data and webhook events before production use. No website can guarantee absolute security.
